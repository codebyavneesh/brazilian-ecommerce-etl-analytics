## Business Problem
A category manager at Olist needs to decide which product categories to
promote and invest in, and which ones to reduce, based on each category's
contribution.

## Business Questions
1. Which categories contribute the most revenue?
2. How many unique customers does each category attract?
3. What share of those customers come back to buy again (repeat purchase rate)?
4. How does each category compare with the other categories and with the
   overall average?

## Metrics
- Revenue per category
- Unique customers per category
- Repeat purchase rate per category
